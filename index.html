Community Standards

Server Rules

Every player must follow our community rules.

01

Respect Everyone

Treat all players and staff respectfully. Harassment and discrimination are prohibited.

02

No RDM or VDM

Random Deathmatch and Vehicle Deathmatch are strictly prohibited.

03

Realistic Roleplay

Stay in character and participate in realistic roleplay scenarios.

04

No Metagaming

Do not use information obtained outside roleplay to gain an in-character advantage.

05

No Powergaming

Do not force unrealistic actions or outcomes onto other players.

06

Follow Staff Instructions

Follow reasonable staff directions and use proper reporting channels.
