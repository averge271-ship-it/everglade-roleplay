Community Guidelines

Server Rules

All players must follow Everglade Roleplay community standards.

  1. Respect all players and staff members.
  2. No Random Deathmatch (RDM).
  3. No Vehicle Deathmatch (VDM).
  4. No FailRP or unrealistic roleplay.
  5. No metagaming or powergaming.
  6. No exploiting, cheating, or hacking.
  7. No harassment, discrimination, or threats.
  8. Follow staff instructions during moderation.
  9. Do not abuse emergency service vehicles or equipment.
  10. Maintain realistic character behavior.
  11. No combat logging to avoid roleplay consequences.
  12. Do not intentionally disrupt active roleplay scenes.
  13. Follow department policies when on duty.
  14. Use voice communications responsibly.
  15. Keep roleplay fair and enjoyable for everyone.

Additional rules and department policies may be posted in Discord.
